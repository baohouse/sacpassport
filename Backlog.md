Parked September 29, 2026.

## 4. Digital stamps instead of a booklet

Parked October 1, 2026. An idea to mull over, mostly for usability.

Collect stamps on sacpassport.com instead of in a printed booklet. The site is already an installable web app, so no app-store download.

**Keep from the booklet:** a person hands out the stamp. The staffed stamp table is the point, because it becomes the festival's welcome desk. Avoid ways to earn a stamp without talking to anyone.

**Ways to stamp:**

- **NFC token held by the staffer.** Tap a phone to it. iPhones from the XS on open the link without an app. NTAG 424 DNA tags give a new signed link on each tap, so a copied link doesn't work.
- **Changing QR code on the staffer's phone.** Backup for phones without NFC.
- **QR poster.** Easy, but a photo can be shared and nobody has to be at the table.
- **Geofence.** Websites can't track location in the background, and driving past would count. At most a check that the tap happened at the venue.

**What digital gains:**

- The "What confused you?" check-in shows right after the stamp, so far more people answer. The newcomer report builds itself.
- Host's Picks can be edited the morning of the event.
- No print runs, refills, COMPLETE stamps, or stock to hold between events.
- Trails and milestones count themselves.

**What it loses:** the physical keepsake, autographs, kids stamping pages, the renewal visit, and people without smartphones.

**Usability questions:**

- **Sign-in.** Safari can delete a site's stored data after about 7 days without a visit, so stamps need an account on a server. How light can sign-in be on a festival field with weak signal: a magic link, a passkey, or nothing until the second stamp?
- **First tap.** What does someone who has never heard of SacPassport see when they tap? Can it work in under 30 seconds?
- **Staffers.** Volunteers and elders running the table need to issue stamps without training or their own phone setup.
- **Fallback.** A rubber stamp on a printed card for kids and anyone without a phone. Does it count toward trails online, and how?
- **Wallet pass.** An Apple or Google Wallet card showing the stamp count, as a keepsake without an app. Apple passes need a $99/year developer account.

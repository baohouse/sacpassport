## 1. Representative restaurants per culture

Parked September 29, 2026.

The best restaurant or restaurants to represent each culture, judged on:

- **Cuisine breadth:** the menu covers the culture's range, not one dish.

- **Vibes:** a room where a newcomer feels the culture, not only eats it.

This gives each culture something to do on a day with no festival.

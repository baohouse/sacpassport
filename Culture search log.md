# Culture search log

Searched peoples stay in this file so a later pass does not look them up again. A row is closed when it already has a SacPassport card, or when a pass already looked for a Greater Sacramento food-and-performance event and did not find one that qualifies.

Scope for a qualifying event: Sacramento, Yolo, Placer, or El Dorado county, plus Lodi (Bao, September 26, 2026). Turlock and Richmond are too far. One people per card. Food tied to that people, plus live music, dance, or performance. cPALSs Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn stay out.

**Last pass:** September 26, 2026. Candidate list: every civilization in Civilization VI (50, through the Leader Pass) and Civilization VII (base game, Crossroads of the World, Right to Rule, Tides of Power, Brush and Blade, and the later Babylon, Gauls, and England additions on the live civilizations page).

Status values: `card`, `closed`, `open`.

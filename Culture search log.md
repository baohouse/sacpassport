# Culture search log

Searched peoples stay in this file so a later pass does not look them up again. A row is closed when it already has a SacPassport card, or when a pass already looked for a Greater Sacramento food-and-performance event and did not find one that qualifies.

Scope for a qualifying event: Sacramento, Yolo, Placer, or El Dorado county, plus Lodi (Bao, September 26, 2026). Bay Area fallback (Bao, September 28, 2026): when a people has no qualifying Greater Sacramento event, its main card may be in the Bay Area from Hayward northward, including Pleasanton, Dublin, and Livermore. That takes in San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley, Richmond, Contra Costa, Marin, Solano, and Napa. Fremont, Union City, San Mateo, and the South Bay stay out. Turlock and Stockton stay out. One people per card. Food tied to that people, plus live music, dance, or performance. cPALSs Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn stay out.

Candidate list: peoples already closed for having no Greater Sacramento event, plus Bay Area diaspora peoples never on a list before (Basque, Tibetan, Burmese, Czech, Finnish, Chilean, and the rows in “Closed September 28, 2026 — Bay Area fallback”).

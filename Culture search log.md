# Culture search log

Searched peoples stay in this file so a later pass does not look them up again. A row is closed when it already has a SacPassport card, or when a pass already looked for a Greater Sacramento food-and-performance event and did not find one that qualifies.

Scope for a qualifying event: Sacramento, Yolo, Placer, or El Dorado county, plus Lodi (Bao, September 26, 2026). Turlock and Richmond are too far. One people per card. Food tied to that people, plus live music, dance, or performance. cPALSs Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn stay out.

**Last pass:** September 28, 2026. Candidate list: Bao’s Hamdeli lead, plus the top 50 countries on Stacker’s [Biggest sources of immigrants to Sacramento](https://stacker.com/stories/california/sacramento/biggest-sources-immigrants-sacramento).

**Pass before:** September 26, 2026. Candidate list: every civilization in Civilization VI (50, through the Leader Pass) and Civilization VII (base game, Crossroads of the World, Right to Rule, Tides of Power, Brush and Blade, and the later Babylon, Gauls, and England additions on the live civilizations page).

Status values: `card`, `closed`, `open`.

## Card added September 28, 2026

Candidate list: Bao’s lead (Hamdeli Festival), plus the countries on Stacker’s [Biggest sources of immigrants to Sacramento](https://stacker.com/stories/california/sacramento/biggest-sources-immigrants-sacramento) (Census 2024 five-year estimates, Sacramento–Roseville–Folsom metro, top 50).

**Afghan** (Stacker #6, 23,695 residents). Eid-ul-Fitr & Nowruz Celebration, Opening Doors, Saturday, April 4, 2026, 11 a.m.–4 p.m., Unitarian Universalist Society of Sacramento, 2425 Sierra Blvd. The English flyer says “Afghani Food & Drinks Provided” and “Enjoy Live Afghani Music,” with three named community performers. Free, RSVP through Eventbrite, capped at 300 registrations. The card estimates March 2027, since Eid al-Fitr moves about 11 days earlier each year. Flyer: `public/flyers/afghan-2026.jpg`, the English page of the three flyers (English, Dari, Pashto) on [Masoud Saqib’s LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/masoud-saqib_we-are-happy-to-announce-that-opening-doors-activity-7440137241517080576-_xA5). Host site: [openingdoorsinc.org](https://openingdoorsinc.org/).

Hamdeli Festival is not on the card. It was one festival, Sunday, August 27, 2023, at Cal Expo, 4–10 p.m., with Aryana Sayeed, Ehsan Aman, Alia Ansari, and Nabi Roshan. Aryana Sayeed’s thank-you post calls it the team’s “very first attempt outside of Afghanistan.” The [@hamdelifestival](https://www.instagram.com/hamdelifestival/) grid stops in August 2023, and hamdelifestival.com no longer resolves. The food line was “diverse cuisines from various food vendors,” not an Afghan menu. The browser rolls a past event to next year’s estimate, so a Hamdeli alternate would show a festival that is not scheduled.

Other Afghan events found: Afghan Mela (Afghan Cultural Society, Weber Point, Stockton) is too far. II&E and Khanae Farhang’s monthly Poetry & Music Night at the Reason Center has live music and no stated food. Opening Doors’ Mosaic (October 8, 2026, B Street Theatre) is a multi-culture night.

**Nicaraguan** (Stacker #33, 1,810 residents). Both events are at El Patio Nica d'Lira, a Nicaraguan restaurant at 4112 Franklin Blvd, Sacramento.

- Main: Festival Nicaragüense, Sunday, April 26, 2026, from 2 p.m. Carlos Mejía Godoy in concert, plus la gigantona y el enano cabezón, and sales of Nicaraguan products. The host billed it as the first Nicaraguan festival in Sacramento. [Facebook event](https://www.facebook.com/events/896899773167642/). The card estimates April 2027.
- Alternate: the restaurant’s first-anniversary party, Saturday, September 26, 2026, from 3 p.m., $25 cover. La Cuneta Son Machín live, Marimba Flor de Sacuanjoche, DJ Chesco from Nicaragua, folk dances, and “la mejor gastronomía nicaragüense.” [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdX7RGnSjYU/). The band’s site lists the date too: [lacunetasonmachin.com](https://lacunetasonmachin.com/).

Flyer: `public/flyers/nicaraguan-2026.jpg`, the Festival Nicaragüense title card at 0:02 of the restaurant’s [promo video](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=2471224586709751) (Bao, September 28, 2026). No still poster was posted: [elpationicadlira.com](https://elpationicadlira.com/) has food photos and a banner only, [Instagram @elpationica](https://www.instagram.com/elpationica/) posts the events as video reels, and the full-size [event](https://www.facebook.com/events/896899773167642/) cover is the restaurant’s generic 1330×492 banner. Both events are Spanish-only.

**Moldovan** (Stacker #18, 5,412 residents). 14th International Mărțișor Festival, Friday, March 6, 2026, 5:30 p.m., Citrus Venue, 6240 San Juan Ave, Citrus Heights. $65, dinner included. Organized by George Culea and Mariana Mikesell, founder of Casa Mare, whose stated mission is “to promote and preserve Moldovan identity and traditions.” Moldova’s Consul General Maxim Arghir attended. The flyer lists mămăligă cu tocăniță, mici cu mazăre, and sarmale cu smântână. [Moldpres](https://www.moldpres.md/rom/cultura/traditiile-de-acasa-celebrate-in-diaspora-festivalul-international-martisor-2026-a-reunit-comunitatea-moldovenilor-si-romanilor-din-california) describes music, folk dance, and the hora. The press line is “moldovenii și românii,” and Mărțișor is shared with Romanians. Casa Mare frames it as Moldovan, and Romanian already has its own card. Flyer: `public/flyers/moldovan-2026.jpg`, from [this Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DU9KfVTgZWc/). The largest size is 640×800. The card estimates March 2027.

**Bosnian** (Stacker #46, 1,099 residents). Card: Dzemat Sacramento Family Night, at the mosque (Bosnian Cultural & Islamic Center), 319 5th Street, Roseville. Saturday-evening ćevapi nights, open to all, held about once a season since 2023: June 3, August 12, and December 2, 2023; October 5 and November 2, 2024; November 22, 2025; February 7, 2026 (“our traditional Family Night”); May 23, 2026. The earlier ones had ćevapi, a bounce house, and games. The folklore group formed in 2025 and gave its first performance at the November 22, 2025 Family Night. The May 23, 2026 flyer lists ćevapi, a folklore performance, and a kids’ program. Flyer: `public/flyers/bosnian-family-night-2026.jpg`, from [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dzematsacramento/p/DX04I9dD1NW/). The card estimates November 2026. Host site: [dzematsacramento.com](https://www.dzematsacramento.com/). The Dzemat [Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/sacramento2019/) has the older Family Night posts.

Not on the card (Bao, September 28, 2026): the 20th Anniversary Gala, October 3, 2026, The Banquet, Fair Oaks, a one-time ticketed dinner with Eldin Huseinbegović. Other Dzemat events: Bosnian Independence Day around March 1 (March 3, 2024: “plenty of delicious Bosnian food” and a program for the occasion; 2026: program and iftar), with no music or dance named. Bajram get-togethers after each Eid (June 22, 2024; June 14, 2025 with ćevapi, cakes, and Bosnian coffee; spring 2025 and 2026) list food only. Srebrenica and White Armband Day commemorations are memorials.

**Chaldean** (Stacker #26, Iraq, 2,671 residents). Chaldean Middle Eastern Festival, Saturday, June 6, 2026, 11 a.m.–10 p.m., free, Our Lady of Perpetual Help Chaldean Catholic Church, 7625 Hazel Ave, Orangevale. The flyer reads “A Celebration of Chaldean Culture & Mediterranean Cuisine” and lists authentic food, live music, and traditional dance, with live music by Alan George. [Sacramento365](https://sacramento365.com/event/chaldean-middle-eastern-festival/) describes “Authentic Chaldean & Middle Eastern food.” Flyer: `public/flyers/chaldean-2026.jpg`, from the Sacramento365 listing; the same flyer is on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DYae0sJAUbV/). The card links the festival’s [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/chaldeanfestivalsac/), because the parish site, oloph.com, fails over https. The card estimates June 2027.

**Lao** (Stacker #10, Laos, 10,867 residents). This reopens the September 23 closure. Lao New Year (Boun Pi Mai) at Wat Lao Saophuth, the “Gerber Temple,” 8741 Gerber Rd, Sacramento. It ran Saturday–Sunday, April 25–26, 2026, 9 a.m.–6 p.m. The 2026 temple flyer lists food and drinks, a marketplace, and the sand pagoda ceremony, but no performance. The 2025 flyer (April 26–27, [reposted on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DI1zH6rTPff/)) lists Lao & Thai street food, cultural dance performances, live music and dancing, and a live band. Flyer: `public/flyers/lao-2026.jpg`, the 2026 temple poster. Host: [Wat Lao Saophuth on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/watlao.saophuth/). The card estimates April 2027. Wat Phosiesattanak billed its April 19–20, 2025 event as a Sacramento Lao New Year, then went back to the shared Lao/Thai/Cambodian New Year on April 18–19, 2026; that one stays off.
